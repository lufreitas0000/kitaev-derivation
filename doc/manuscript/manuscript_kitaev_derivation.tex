\begin{abstract}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: Can exact symbolic Schrieffer-Wolff projector reductions and an independent 66-state Fock oracle eliminate representation drift in deriving microscopic $(J,K,\Gamma,\Gamma')$ interactions?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: Jackeli2009, RauLeeKee2014, Winter2016
% CLAIM: Theorem-And-Oracle-Verified (M2.1)
We present the exact symbolic Schrieffer--Wolff projector derivation and independent $66$-state two-hole Fock-space verification implemented in \texttt{kitaev-derivation}~\cite{Jackeli2009,RauLeeKee2014,Winter2016}. Operating cleanly within the $t_{2g}$ $d^5$--$d^4$--$d^6$ Hubbard--Kanamori manifold, the Python/\texttt{SymPy} engine establishes exact $C_2$ and $C_3$ bond covariance, $J_H=0$ destructive interference, the Jackeli--Khaliullin and Rau--Lee--Kee closed-form $(J, K, \Gamma, \Gamma')$ tensors, and machine-precision parity ($<10^{-16}$) against an independent \texttt{scipy.sparse} Fock oracle in the $\lambda_{\mathrm{int}}=0$ intermediate-state limit, while separately quantifying finite-$\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}}$ intermediate-state denominator shifts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and Strong-Coupling Reduction}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: How do exact multiplet projectors $P_{[L=0,1,2]}$ map hopping channels into $(J,K,\Gamma,\Gamma')$ tensors?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: Jackeli2009, RauLeeKee2014
% CLAIM: Mathematical-Identity (KD-001)
For edge-shared octahedral transition-metal ions ($d^5$, one $t_{2g}$ hole per site), the unperturbed atomic problem splits into the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ Kramers doublet ground state and the $15$-dimensional two-hole $d^4$ Hubbard--Kanamori multiplets $P_{[L=0,1,2]}$ with excitation gaps $\Delta E_0 = U + 2J_H$, $\Delta E_1 = U - 3J_H$, and $\Delta E_2 = U - J_H$~\cite{Jackeli2009,RauLeeKee2014}.

Second-order canonical Schrieffer--Wolff reduction yields
\begin{equation}
  H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(2)} = -\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_\gamma}\sum_{L=0}^{2}\frac{1}{\Delta E_L} P_{\mathrm{low}}\,T_{-1}\,P_{i,[L]}^{(2)}\,T_1\,P_{\mathrm{low}} + (i \leftrightarrow j),
\end{equation}
whose projection onto $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2 \otimes j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ yields $H_{\mathrm{spin}} = \sum_{\alpha\beta} J_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)} S_i^\alpha S_j^\beta$ (equivalently $K_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)} = J_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)}/4$ in the Pauli $\sigma_i^\alpha\sigma_j^\beta$ convention of \texttt{qsl\_conventions.json}).

\section{Closed-Form Verification and Finite-SOC Departures}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: Does the 66-state sparse Fock oracle verify both exact lambda_int=0 analytic expressions and finite-lambda_SOC corrections?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: RauLeeKee2014, Winter2016
% CLAIM: Oracle-Verified (KD-002)
In the pure ligand-mediated limit ($t_1=t_3=t_4=0$), the $L=0$ channel vanishes inside the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ doublet and destructive interference between $L=1$ and $L=2$ enforces $J=\Gamma=\Gamma'=0$ with $K = -\frac{8J_H t_2^2}{3(U-3J_H)(U-J_H)}$~\cite{Jackeli2009}. Switching on direct hopping ($t_1, t_3, t_4$) yields exact $C_3$-covariant $\Gamma(t_4=0) = \frac{16J_H t_2 (t_1 - t_3)}{9(U-J_H)(U-3J_H)}$ and nonzero $\Gamma' \propto t_4$~\cite{RauLeeKee2014}. As documented in Table~\ref{tab:ms-fock}, all symbolic expressions match the $\lambda_{\mathrm{int}}=0$ $66$-state two-hole Fock oracle $(J_{\mathrm{Fock}}^{\lambda_0}, K_{\mathrm{Fock}}^{\lambda_0})$ to $\max|\Delta_{\lambda_0}| < 10^{-16}$; for \texttt{RuCl3\_lam\_0p15} ($\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}} = 0.15$ eV~\cite{Winter2016}), the final three columns separately isolate the finite-SOC intermediate-state denominator shift $(J_{\mathrm{Fock}}^{\mathrm{SOC}}, K_{\mathrm{Fock}}^{\mathrm{SOC}})$.

\section{Fourth-Order Bond Exchange and Hexagon Ring Exchange (\texttt{M2.2})}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: Do finite-SOC fourth-order processes and 6-site Peierls pi-flux ring exchange match non-perturbative BDL direct rotation across idealized material regimes?
% SOURCE [EXECUTED]: src/kitaev_derivation/services/fourth_order_exchange.py, data/exports/extended_kitaev_material_couplings.dat
% CLAIM: Oracle-Verified (KD-003, KD-004)
At fourth order in inter-site hopping $t$, two-site BDL perturbation theory separates cleanly into wavefunction charge-transfer renormalization $\frac{1}{2}\{(V_{PQ}R_Q^2V_{QP}),(V_{PQ}R_QV_{QP})\}$ and a neutral $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=3/2$ intermediate chain $-V_{PQ}R_QV_{QQ}R_QV_{QQ}R_QV_{QP}$ that activates when $\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}} < \infty$. Across idealized $\alpha\text{-RuCl}_3$ and $\text{Na}_2\text{IrO}_3$ parameter sets (Table~\ref{tab:ms-m22}), analytical fourth-order tensors match non-perturbative $3$-point Richardson BDL direct rotation to $\le 7.41\times 10^{-10}$. On the $6$-site half-filled Kramers hexagon ($\binom{12}{6}=924$ states), threading a Peierls $\pi$-flux isolates the genuine six-spin ring exchange $\frac{1}{2}(H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(6)}(0) - H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(6)}(\pi))$ from all lower-order disconnected diagrams ($\le 7.31\times 10^{-7}$ vs non-perturbative BDL), while the formal Lean~4 CAR and multiplet resolvent bridge resides in open PR~\texttt{finite-QMB\#52}.
