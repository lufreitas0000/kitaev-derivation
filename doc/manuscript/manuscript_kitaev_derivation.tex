\begin{abstract}
We present the exact symbolic Schrieffer--Wolff projector derivation and independent $66$-state two-hole Fock-space verification implemented in \texttt{kitaev-derivation}. Operating cleanly within the $t_{2g}$ $d^5$--$d^4$--$d^6$ Hubbard--Kanamori manifold, the Python/\texttt{SymPy} engine establishes exact $C_2$ and $C_3$ bond covariance, $J_H=0$ destructive interference, the Jackeli--Khaliullin and Rau--Lee--Kee closed-form $(J, K, \Gamma, \Gamma')$ tensors, and machine-precision parity ($<10^{-13}$) against an independent \texttt{scipy.sparse} Fock oracle, while quantifying finite-$\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}}$ intermediate-state denominator shifts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and Strong-Coupling Reduction}
For edge-shared octahedral transition-metal ions ($d^5$, one $t_{2g}$ hole per site), the unperturbed atomic problem splits into the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ Kramers doublet ground state and the $15$-dimensional two-hole $d^4$ Hubbard--Kanamori multiplets $P_{[L=0,1,2]}$ with excitation gaps $\Delta E_0 = U + 2J_H$, $\Delta E_1 = U - 3J_H$, and $\Delta E_2 = U - J_H$.

Second-order canonical Schrieffer--Wolff reduction yields
\begin{equation}
  H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(2)} = -\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_\gamma}\sum_{L=0}^{2}\frac{1}{\Delta E_L} P_{\mathrm{low}}\,T_{-1}\,P_{i,[L]}^{(2)}\,T_1\,P_{\mathrm{low}} + (i \leftrightarrow j),
\end{equation}
whose projection onto $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2 \otimes j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ yields $H_{\mathrm{spin}} = \sum_{\alpha\beta} J_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)} S_i^\alpha S_j^\beta$ (equivalently $K_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)} = J_{\alpha\beta}^{(\gamma)}/4$ in the Pauli $\sigma_i^\alpha\sigma_j^\beta$ convention of \texttt{qsl\_conventions.json}).

\section{Closed-Form Verification and Finite-SOC Departures}
In the pure ligand-mediated limit ($t_1=t_3=t_4=0$), the $L=0$ channel vanishes inside the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ doublet and destructive interference between $L=1$ and $L=2$ enforces $J=\Gamma=\Gamma'=0$ with $K = -\frac{8J_H t_2^2}{3(U-3J_H)(U-J_H)}$. Switching on direct hopping ($t_1, t_3, t_4$) yields exact $C_3$-covariant $\Gamma(t_4=0) = \frac{16J_H t_2 (t_1 - t_3)}{9(U-J_H)(U-3J_H)}$ and nonzero $\Gamma' \propto t_4$. As documented in Table~\ref{tab:ms-fock}, all symbolic expressions match the independent $66$-state two-hole Fock oracle to $\max|\Delta| < 10^{-16}$.
