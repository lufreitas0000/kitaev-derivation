\chapter{Hubbard--Kanamori Multiplets, \texorpdfstring{$T$--$P$}{T-P} SOC, and CAR Algebra}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: How do exact CAR normal-ordering, Kanamori P_[L=0,1,2] projectors, and the 66-state Fock-space oracle eliminate dual-representation drift?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: Jackeli2009, RauLeeKee2014
% CLAIM: Theorem-And-Oracle-Verified (M2.1)

In the $t_{2g}$ edge-shared octahedral hole representation ($d^5$ single-hole ground state, $d^4$ two-hole virtual intermediates of dimension $\binom{6}{2}=15$), $T$--$P$ equivalence $\mathbf{L}_{t_{2g}} = -\mathbf{l}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ yields $H_{\mathrm{SOC}} = +\lambda \mathbf{S}\cdot\mathbf{l}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with Kramers $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ doublet eigenenergy $-\lambda$ and $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=3/2$ quartet at $+\lambda/2$~\cite{Jackeli2009}.

Under the spherically symmetric Hubbard--Kanamori Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
  V = \sum_i \left[\frac{U-3J_H}{2}(\bar{N}_i - 1)^2 - 2J_H\mathbf{S}_i^2 - \frac{J_H}{2}\mathbf{L}_i^2\right],
\end{equation}
the $15$ two-hole intermediate states resolve into $\Delta E_{L=1} = U - 3J_H$ ($9$-fold $^3P$), $\Delta E_{L=2} = U - J_H$ ($5$-fold $^1D$), and $\Delta E_{L=0} = U + 2J_H$ ($1$-fold $^1S$).

\chapter{Symbolic Extraction vs.\ Independent 66-State Fock Oracle}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: Do exact SymPy Schrieffer-Wolff reductions agree identically across C3 bonds with exact 66-state Fock projection and finite-SOC corrections?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: Jackeli2009, RauLeeKee2014, Winter2016
% CLAIM: Oracle-Verified (KD-002)

For general $z$-bond hopping $T_z(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4)$, right-to-left multiplet projection onto $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2 \otimes j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ recovers the Jackeli--Khaliullin coupling $K = -\frac{8 J_H t_2^2}{3(U-3J_H)(U-J_H)}$ at $t_1=t_3=t_4=0$ (spin-$1/2$ units; $K/4$ in Pauli $\sigma^\gamma\sigma^\gamma$ units)~\cite{Jackeli2009} and $\Gamma(t_4=0) = \frac{16 J_H t_2 (t_1 - t_3)}{9(U-J_H)(U-3J_H)}$~\cite{RauLeeKee2014}. Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-fock} compares symbolic CAS outputs directly against the independent $66$-state two-hole \texttt{scipy.sparse} Fock-space oracle at $\lambda_{\mathrm{int}}=0$ ($\max|\Delta_{\lambda_0}| < 10^{-16}$) alongside the finite-$\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}}$ intermediate-state denominator shift for $\alpha$-RuCl$_3$-like parameters~\cite{Winter2016}.

\chapter{Fourth-Order BDL Exchange and 924-State Hexagon Ring Exchange}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: How strongly do finite-SOC j=3/2 intermediates renormalize fourth-order Kitaev couplings, and how does Peierls pi-flux isolate 6-site hexagon ring exchange?
% SOURCE [EXECUTED]: src/kitaev_derivation/services/fourth_order_exchange.py, tests/test_fourth_order_exchange.py
% CLAIM: Oracle-Verified (KD-003, KD-004)

In the $66$-state two-hole Fock space, partitioning $Q_0 = I_{66} - P_0$ retains both the $30$ charge-transfer states and the $32$ neutral spin-orbit excited $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=3/2$ states when $\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}} > 0$ (reducing cleanly to the $34$-state projected charge complement in the $\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}} \to \infty$ limit). Under the Hermitian Bravyi--DiVincenzo--Loss / Takahashi fourth-order expansion
\begin{equation}
  H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(4)} = -V_{PQ} R_Q V_{QQ} R_Q V_{QQ} R_Q V_{QP}
  + \frac{1}{2}\Big\{(V_{PQ} R_Q^2 V_{QP}),\; (V_{PQ} R_Q V_{QP})\Big\},
\end{equation}
three-point Richardson elimination against non-perturbative BDL direct rotation confirms $\max|H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(4)} - H_{\mathrm{BDL}}^{(4)}| \le 7.41 \times 10^{-10}$ across representative idealized $\alpha$-RuCl$_3$ and Na$_2$IrO$_3$ model points (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-m22}). Crucially, at finite $\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}}=0.15$, the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=3/2$ chain channel $-V_{PQ} R_Q V_{QQ} R_Q V_{QQ} R_Q V_{QP}$ enhances ferromagnetic $J_4 = -0.0017968$ while shifting $K_4 = +0.0009718$. On a $6$-site half-filled Kramers hexagon ($\binom{12}{6}=924$ states), subtracting Peierls fluxes $\frac{1}{2}(H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(6)}(0) - H_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(6)}(\pi))$ cancels all disconnected two- and four-bond renormalization diagrams identically, matching $3$-point Richardson BDL direct rotation to $\le 7.31 \times 10^{-7}$.
