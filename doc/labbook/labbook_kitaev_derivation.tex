\chapter{Hubbard--Kanamori Multiplets, \texorpdfstring{$T$--$P$}{T-P} SOC, and CAR Algebra}
% EDITORIAL QUESTION: How do exact CAR normal-ordering, Kanamori P_[L=0,1,2] projectors, and the 66-state Fock-space oracle eliminate dual-representation drift?
% SOURCE [VERIFIED]: Jackeli2009, RauLeeKee2014
% CLAIM: Theorem-And-Oracle-Verified (M2.1)

In the $t_{2g}$ edge-shared octahedral hole representation ($d^5$ single-hole ground state, $d^4$ two-hole virtual intermediates of dimension $\binom{6}{2}=15$), $T$--$P$ equivalence $\mathbf{L}_{t_{2g}} = -\mathbf{l}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ yields $H_{\mathrm{SOC}} = +\lambda \mathbf{S}\cdot\mathbf{l}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with Kramers $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ doublet eigenenergy $-\lambda$ and $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=3/2$ quartet at $+\lambda/2$.

Under the spherically symmetric Hubbard--Kanamori Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
  V = \sum_i \left[\frac{U-3J_H}{2}(\bar{N}_i - 1)^2 - 2J_H\mathbf{S}_i^2 - \frac{J_H}{2}\mathbf{L}_i^2\right],
\end{equation}
the $15$ two-hole intermediate states resolve into $\Delta E_{L=1} = U - 3J_H$ ($9$-fold $^3P$), $\Delta E_{L=2} = U - J_H$ ($5$-fold $^1D$), and $\Delta E_{L=0} = U + 2J_H$ ($1$-fold $^1S$).

\chapter{Symbolic Extraction vs.\ Independent 66-State Fock Oracle}

For general $z$-bond hopping $T_z(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4)$, right-to-left multiplet projection onto $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2 \otimes j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ recovers the Jackeli--Khaliullin coupling $K = -\frac{8 J_H t_2^2}{3(U-3J_H)(U-J_H)}$ at $t_1=t_3=t_4=0$ (spin-$1/2$ units; $K/4$ in Pauli $\sigma^\gamma\sigma^\gamma$ units) and $\Gamma(t_4=0) = \frac{16 J_H t_2 (t_1 - t_3)}{9(U-J_H)(U-3J_H)}$.
