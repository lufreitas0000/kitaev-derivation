- M2.1: Pure-Python SymPy projector CAS and 66-state Fock oracle, symbolic second-order bond coefficients verified numerically to <1e-13 in the SOC-projector / charge-denominator approximation.
- M2.2: Fourth-order BDL/Takahashi isolated-bond coefficients, including numerical finite-SOC neutral channels, checked against Richardson direct rotation. Separate 924-state phenomenological Kramers Hubbard hexagon winding calculation; its Peierls flux-sensitive operator is not the complete microscopic multiorbital sixth-order Hamiltonian. Historical evidence remains in PROJECT_STATE.yaml; scope/terminology and ring convention audits tracked in #4 and #8.

## Horizon 2 — Near-future Wave 4: electrically responsive generalized t2g model

Scientific objective: derive a general microscopic spin Hamiltonian AND dressed charge operators, with independently verified limits and explicit applicability domains. Retain three spatial t2g orbitals (six spin-orbitals) only; begin in the existing SOC-projector approximation. Arbitrary local onsite energy difference $\delta_{ij}=\epsilon_i-\epsilon_j$ and independent signed second-neighbor $t_2'$ are first-class inputs.

### Near-term scientific deliverables
- [ ] **N1 — Voltage-biased exchange / DM** ([#2](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/2)): symbolic second-order full $3\times3$ tensor for scalar local potentials, all J,K,Gamma,Gamma', D components, directed denominators, bias parity, zero-bias recovery and exact/null symmetry tests; 66-state oracle. Then biased fourth-order isolated bond and numerical finite-SOC checks. Do not require nonzero DM.
- [ ] **N2 — Direct NNN and three-site connected exchange** ([#5](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/5)): dominant orbital $t_2'$ following Pereira–Egger and Freitas et al.; direct second-order two-site NNN tensor, 816-state three-site Fock basis and mixed third-order triangular paths; fourth-order connected adjacent-bond processes and subcluster subtraction. Treat each slice separately; density work needs triangle infrastructure, not completed fourth order.
- [ ] **N3 — General dressed charge-density operator** ([#6](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/6)): $P e^S(N_i-1)e^{-S}P$ with bias-induced second-order and cubic NN–NNN terms, reproduce both published derivations and generalize to all specified hoppings including $t_4$, signs and site potentials. Exact operator neutrality, transform in consistent SW gauge, independent Fock operator/expectation checks and exported coefficients. No dependency on finite-SOC symbolic backlog.

### Cross-cutting scientific and verification tasks
- [ ] **Q1 — Convention and scope correction** ([#4](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/4)): exact local vs global/isolated cluster particle-number statements and Hilbert dimensions; bare product Kramers projector vs SW dressing; avoid premature projected hopping; replace misleading infSOC/lam_soc=0 selector with explicit SOC-projector / charge-denominator mode; distinguish spin/Pauli, axes, hole/electron and units. Preserve legacy results/provenance.
- [ ] **Q2 — Energy hierarchy and perturbative validity** ([#7](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/7)): factor exact symbolic hopping monomials from atomic denominators; signed parameter tables and absolute-scale sorting, exact charge/SOC gaps and dimensionless ratios; reproducible physical-hopping direct-rotation comparisons, gap/overlap tracking, individual couplings and residuals. Do not conflate coefficient parity with convergence or RG stability.
- [ ] **Q3 — Hexagon audit and safe exports** ([#8](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/kitaev-derivation/issues/8)): audit suspected site-axis mismatch of W_p; project correct Pauli string and quantify residual operators; establish what Peierls subtraction computes; clearly label phenomenological reduced-model inputs and any unresolved corrections. No unqualified downstream physical interpretation of ring coefficients until the relevant audit is accepted.

Suggested sequence: Q1/Q2 and N1 first; N2 direct and cluster infrastructure can proceed alongside N1; N3 once the bias/triangle prerequisites are available; complete higher-order slices independently. Q3 should gate interpretation of ring exports, not block the second-order core. Maintain existing dual verification and publication gates. Update the current sprint/machine state only when the next milestone is activated.
